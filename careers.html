Finance and internal control

Finance & Internal Control Manager

Lead budgeting, cash-flow planning and financial reporting; strengthen controls and compliance, oversee audit follow-up and fraud prevention, assess risk, and advise management while supervising staff.

Essential fit

  • Bachelor's degree in Social Sciences.
  • Substantial finance, control, risk or compliance experience in a commercial bank, consultancy or corporate organisation.
  • Experience in a comparable management role or senior banking position, with strong leadership, analysis, communication and professional integrity.

Added advantage

  • Certified Fraud Examiner (CFE), or experience in financial crime compliance, KYC, credit assessment or banking operations.
  • Business management or senior advisory experience; postgraduate study in governance, development or a related field.
Entry level

Junior SAS Programmer

Support the team in writing and checking SAS programs for clinical datasets, tables, listings and figures.

Good fit if you have

  • Basic SAS knowledge from study, projects or work.
  • A foundation in statistics, maths, life sciences or computing.
  • Careful attention to detail and an appetite to learn CDISC and clinical research processes.
Experienced

Senior SAS Programmer

Lead programming work, review outputs and help teams deliver reliable study datasets and reporting to agreed timelines.

Good fit if you have

  • Strong SAS programming experience in clinical research.
  • Practical knowledge of SDTM, ADaM and CDISC standards.
  • Experience with QC, documentation, review and cross-functional study teams.
Statistics

Biostatistician

Contribute to study design, statistical methods, analysis plans, interpretation and clear communication of results.

Good fit if you have

  • Training or equivalent experience in statistics or biostatistics.
  • Familiarity with SAS or R and clinical study methodology.
  • The ability to explain statistical decisions to multidisciplinary teams.
Data science

Data Scientist

Explore clinical or research data, build reproducible analyses and communicate useful findings to project teams.

Good fit if you have

  • Working knowledge of Python or R, statistics and data visualisation.
  • Experience preparing, analysing and validating structured data.
  • Clear communication; experience with health data and privacy practices is valuable.
Clinical programming

Statistical Programmer

Develop and validate analysis datasets and tables, listings and figures in line with study specifications.

Good fit if you have

  • Programming experience with SAS or R and strong data skills.
  • Understanding of clinical trial data and CDISC standards.
  • A methodical approach to QC, traceability and working with statisticians.
Clinical data

Clinical Data Manager

Coordinate study data workflows, review data quality, resolve queries and support database closeout.

Good fit if you have

  • Experience with clinical data management or electronic data capture systems.
  • Careful query management, reconciliation and documentation habits.
  • Awareness of clinical data standards, confidentiality and study timelines.
